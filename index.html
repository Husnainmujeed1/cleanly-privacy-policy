Privacy Policy

1. Short version

  • Photos, videos, contacts, and calendar events are read on your device so the app can find items you may want to clean. We do not upload that content to our servers.
  • Secret Space keeps hidden photos and contacts in the app’s private storage, protected by a PIN and, if you turn it on, the biometrics already on your device. We cannot see it and we cannot recover a forgotten PIN.
  • You do not need an account. There are no ads and we do not collect the advertising ID.
  • We do receive product-usage events through Google Firebase Analytics, and subscription status through Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat.

2. What stays on your phone

These categories are processed locally:

  • Photos and videos. With your permission, Cleanly scans your library to group similar and duplicate photos, blurry photos, screenshots, and video groups such as large, short, screen, and duplicate recordings. Compression and swipe review also happen on the device. Files are deleted only when you confirm, using the system photo library.
  • Contacts. With your permission, Cleanly reads contacts to find duplicates and incomplete entries, and can write changes you confirm (for example a merge). A copy you choose to hide is stored only inside Secret Space.
  • Calendar. With your permission, Cleanly reads events so you can review and delete the ones you select. Deleted events are removed from the calendar on the device.
  • Installed apps. On Android, Cleanly can list launchable apps you installed so you can open the system uninstall screen. That list is not uploaded.
  • Storage numbers. Total and free disk space are read so the app can show how much room you have. Those figures stay in the session.
  • Secret Space. A key is stored in the system Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android). The vault index is encrypted on the device. Hidden files live in the app’s private folder, are excluded from Android backup, and are kept out of iCloud backup. Biometric unlock uses the sensor and secure hardware the operating system already provides. We never receive your PIN, fingerprint, or face data.
  • Settings. Choices such as theme and whether you finished onboarding are stored on the device.

3. Permissions

The operating system asks before Cleanly can use sensitive access. You can deny a permission and still use the parts of the app that do not need it. You can revoke access later in system settings.

  • Photos and videos — to scan, compress, and delete media you choose.
  • Contacts — to find duplicates or incomplete contacts and apply edits you confirm.
  • Calendar — to list events and delete the ones you confirm.
  • Biometrics — only if you turn on biometric unlock for Secret Space.
  • Internet — for analytics, subscription status, and opening store or mail links.

4. What leaves your phone

Usage analytics. After the app starts, Firebase Analytics records events about how the product is used: screens you open, buttons you tap, onboarding progress, and whether a purchase completed, was cancelled, or restored. Collection of the advertising ID is turned off, and ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization are not allowed. Analytics does not include your photos, videos, contacts, calendar, or Secret Space contents.

Purchases. If you subscribe, Apple App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We do not receive your card number. RevenueCat tells the app whether a premium entitlement is active so locked features can unlock, including on a new install when you tap Restore.

Email you send us. Contact Us opens your own mail app. If you send a message, it includes what you typed plus a short diagnostics block: app name and version, bundle id, platform and OS version, device model, language, the time of the report, and how long the app had been open. It does not include media or your contacts. We receive that email only because you send it.

5. How we use it

  • To run the cleaner, compressor, swipe review, contacts, calendar, and Secret Space features on your device.
  • To understand which screens and actions people use, and to fix problems.
  • To know whether your subscription is active and to restore it.
  • To read and reply to support mail you choose to send.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your library to train models off the device, and we do not build an advertising profile.

6. Who we share it with

We share only the limited data described above, and only with:

  • Google Firebase — usage analytics. Google’s processing is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple or Google — the store that bills you and hosts the app.
  • Your email provider — only for messages you send from Contact Us.

These providers may process data in the United States or other countries where they operate. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Ads

Cleanly does not show ads and does not include an advertising SDK. The Android advertising-ID permission is removed from the app.

8. Children

Cleanly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child sent us personal information, email khan.mobileapps@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. How long we keep it

  • Library, contacts, calendar, and Secret Space data remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s private storage, including Secret Space files. On iOS, a Keychain item can sometimes survive uninstall; it cannot bring the deleted files back.
  • Analytics is kept according to your Firebase project’s retention settings.
  • Support emails are kept as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of billing questions, then deleted.

10. Security

Secret Space uses on-device encryption and the operating system’s key storage. No method of storage or transmission is perfect. You are responsible for your PIN and for who can unlock your phone.

11. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke photos, contacts, calendar, or biometrics in system settings.
  • Skip any cleaner category you do not want scanned.
  • Cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account. The app’s Settings screen can open that page when a subscription exists.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its local data.
  • Email khan.mobileapps@gmail.com to ask what support mail we still have, or to ask us to delete it. Analytics events are not tied to your name; we generally cannot find and delete a single person’s historical analytics.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Write to us and we will respond as the law that applies to you requires.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and post the new version at this page. Continued use of the app after the new date means you accept the update.
